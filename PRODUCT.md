# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are homeowners, resort owners, and property managers on or near the Placencia Peninsula who are evaluating a landscaping, stonework, garden, or property-care project.

## Product Purpose

The site helps prospective clients understand Placencia Greenscapes and Stone's services, see evidence of the work, meet Edison, and start a project conversation through WhatsApp or phone.

## Positioning

The business combines tropical landscape knowledge, local stone craftsmanship, and hands-on project ownership in one service across the Placencia peninsula.

## Operating Context

Visitors are often evaluating a property improvement from a phone, a resort or home office, or while traveling in Belize. They need to recognize the business quickly, understand the kind of work it does, see credible visual proof, and contact Edison without a long form workflow.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-page marketing site with anchored sections and a not-found route.
- Primary conversion paths are WhatsApp at +501 612-2111 and direct phone contact.
- The contact form opens a prefilled WhatsApp message; it does not submit to a backend.
- Existing image assets and factual copy are the source of truth; do not fabricate project claims, clients, pricing, or certifications.
- The experience must remain responsive, keyboard accessible, reduced-motion aware, and deployable as a static Vite build to Cloudflare Pages.

## Brand Commitments

- Business name: Placencia Greenscapes and Stone.
- Location: Placencia, Belize and the surrounding peninsula.
- Edison is the owner and lead craftsman.
- Preserve the existing logo and real project/portrait imagery.
- Voice should feel confident, grounded, warm, and specific to tropical outdoor work.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing photography in `public/images/` for hero, gardens, stonework, tree maintenance, irrigation, lawn care, hardscaping, tropical garden, and Edison.
- Existing service descriptions, testimonial copy, service-area list, and contact details in the React components.
- Existing Cloudflare Pages deployment and WhatsApp/phone conversion paths.

## Product Principles

- Show the work before asking for the lead.
- Make local expertise concrete and memorable.
- Keep the path to WhatsApp or a phone call obvious.
- Respect the visitor's time on mobile.
- Use honest evidence instead of decorative claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain semantic landmarks, visible keyboard focus, usable contrast, touch targets, descriptive image text, and a complete reduced-motion path.
